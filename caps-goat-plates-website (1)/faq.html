Two things: proof of identity (photocard licence, or passport plus a recent utility bill) and proof of entitlement to the registration (V5C, V5C/2 new keeper slip, V750 or V778). You send both after ordering — it takes about two minutes. Full detail on our legal and documents page.
Order by 2pm on a working day with your documents verified and we dispatch the same day, tracked. Delivery is free on orders over £30, otherwise £3.95.
Most UK cars take the standard 520 × 111 mm oblong. 4×4s with a rear-mounted spare often need the 279 × 203 mm square, and motorcycles registered after 1 September 2001 take a 229 × 178 mm rear plate with no front plate. See our sizes and fitment guide.
3D gel characters are cast in resin and domed, giving a soft, glossy rise of roughly one to two millimetres. 4D characters are laser-cut from 3 mm acrylic with sharp vertical edges and a hard shadow. 4D gel combines both — an acrylic core with a gel crown on top.
