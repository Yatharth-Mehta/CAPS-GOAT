Why The Goat

Pressed Different.

Pressed, not printed-and-prayed

Every plate is built by hand on components certified to BS AU 145e — the current British Standard — then checked twice before it leaves the bench.

Legal, sorted

We’re a DVLA-registered supplier. Send your ID and proof of entitlement and your plates arrive road-ready, MOT-proof, with the supplier marking exactly where the law wants it.

24-hour dispatch

Order by 2pm with your documents verified and your plates are pressed, packed and gone the same day — tracked to your door anywhere in the UK.

The GOAT Guarantee

If a character ever peels, cracks or fades, we remake the plate free. Not for three years — for the life of the plate. No small print, no drama.

How It Works

Four Steps. Done.

1

Build it in the Lab

Type your registration, choose your finish, badge, border and size. Front and rear render live as you go — what you see is what we press.

2

Send your documents

UK law needs proof of who you are and proof the registration is yours. Photograph both, send them over, and we verify by hand — usually within the hour.

3

We press and check

Your plate is made to order on certified components, inspected against your build, and packed with protective film and a fixing kit if you added one.

4

Tracked to your door

Dispatched the same working day when ordered by 2pm. Free UK delivery over £30, fully tracked, usually with you next day.

One minute. One plate. Yours.
